The dashboard is mission control. Click the CineTray icon and choose Open dashboard. On the left you will find every playlist with its own color and count. Pick one to browse its videos, rename it, drag rows to reorder them, or remove anything you do not want. Open All launches up to twelve videos at once. You can search across every playlist from the box on the left, and export or import your whole collection as a single JSON file. Settings let you fine tune the hover chip, player dragging, the undo button, which side the dock slides from, and the animations.
